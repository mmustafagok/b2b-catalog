# CatalogFlow — App Store Screenshot Checklist & Assets

## Specifications
- **Format:** PNG / JPEG
- **Dimensions:** 1280 × 800 px (16:10) or 1920 × 1080 px (16:9)
- **Content:** Real UI data only; no lorem ipsum; no internal IDs or tokens; no debug panels.

---

## Required Screenshots

### 1. Merchant Overview Dashboard
- **Location:** Embedded Admin Overview (`/app`)
- **Visual:** Active catalog cards, commercial funnel metrics (Catalog Views $\rightarrow$ Draft Orders Created), monthly quota progress bar, and recent buyer submissions table.
- **Key Caption:** "Real-time wholesale overview: track buyer engagement, conversion funnels, and draft order quotas."

### 2. Wholesale Catalog Creation & Rules
- **Location:** Catalog Creation Modal / Form
- **Visual:** Catalog name, wholesale % discount selector, collection and product source selectors, SKU and inventory display toggles.
- **Key Caption:** "Curate wholesale catalogs in seconds using collections, explicit products, or percentage discounts."

### 3. Product & Collection Sourcing
- **Location:** Sourced Collection / Product Selector
- **Visual:** Clean list of mirrored Shopify products and smart collections available for inclusion.
- **Key Caption:** "Automatically synchronize with Shopify inventory and smart collections."

### 4. Wholesale Buyer Portal (Public View)
- **Location:** Buyer Portal (`/c/:token`)
- **Visual:** Clean wholesale catalog banner, real-time search bar, brand styling, and product catalog cards.
- **Key Caption:** "Share unlisted wholesale links with buyers—no account or password required."

### 5. High-Velocity Variant Matrix Ordering
- **Location:** Buyer Portal Variant Matrix Expanded
- **Visual:** Tabular grid showing variant options (e.g. Size, Color), wholesale discounted prices, live inventory badges, and bulk quantity inputs.
- **Key Caption:** "Fast bulk ordering: buyers input quantities across variants in a streamlined matrix."

### 6. Sticky Order Summary & Wholesale Cart
- **Location:** Bottom of Buyer Portal
- **Visual:** Floating sticky summary bar with total variant lines, total items, subtotal calculation, and "Review & Submit" CTA.
- **Key Caption:** "Instant totals: sticky summary bar calculates line quantities and wholesale savings in real time."

### 7. Native Shopify Draft Order
- **Location:** Shopify Admin $\rightarrow$ Orders $\rightarrow$ Drafts
- **Visual:** Resulting Draft Order in Shopify Admin with line items, wholesale discounts, buyer email, PO number, and CatalogFlow tags.
- **Key Caption:** "Turn buyer orders directly into native Shopify Draft Orders ready for merchant invoicing."

### 8. Submissions History & Deep Links
- **Location:** Merchant Admin $\rightarrow$ Submissions Tab
- **Visual:** Paginated history of buyer submissions with catalog names, items, currency subtotals, and direct "Open in Shopify ↗" links.
- **Key Caption:** "Full submission traceability with one-click deep links directly into Shopify Admin."

### 9. Predictable Billing & Commercial Quotas
- **Location:** Merchant Admin $\rightarrow$ Billing & Plans Tab
- **Visual:** Commercial plan comparison cards (Starter, Growth, Scale), quota utilization bars, and hard-cap predictability.
- **Key Caption:** "Predictable wholesale plans with 7-day trials, hard caps, and zero surprise overages."
